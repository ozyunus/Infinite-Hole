# Infinite Hole Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Infinite Hole does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising,
analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, or third-party data collection. Game
progress is used only during the current play session and is not sent to the
developer or any third party.

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those
changes are released.

For privacy questions, contact `ozyunus@msn.com`.
