# Infinite Hole Support

Infinite Hole is a minimalist reflex game for iPhone and iPad.

## How to play

Drag horizontally to move the hole. Catch the falling shapes that fit inside
it. The pace increases as you catch more shapes.

## Contact

For help, bug reports, or feedback, email `ozyunus@msn.com` or open an issue in
the [Infinite Hole repository](https://github.com/ozyunus/Infinite-Hole/issues).

When reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS version, and a short
description of what happened.
