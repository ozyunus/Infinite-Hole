2. Purpose, target audience, and value

Infinite Hole: Geometry is a casual arcade and reflex game for a general
audience. The player moves a hole horizontally to catch falling geometric
shapes that fit. It provides short, distraction-free play sessions focused on
timing, positioning, hand-eye coordination, and reacting to an increasing game
speed.

3. Setup and access instructions

No setup, account, login credentials, subscription, sample files, or network
connection is required. Launch the app, tap PLAY, and drag horizontally on the
game area to move the hole. Shapes small enough to fit can be caught. The
counters at the top show possible shapes, caught shapes, and the current level.
Tap EXIT to return to the title screen.

4. External services, tools, or platforms

The app does not use external services to deliver its core functionality. It
has no backend, authentication service, analytics service, advertising SDK,
payment processor, data provider, AI service, or external API. Gameplay runs
locally on the device. The app is developed with Flutter and the Flame game
engine.
